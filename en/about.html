About Lucky Turtle Saju

Lucky Turtle Saju (느릿느릿 사주풀이, "slow and steady Saju") is an independent website about the Korean Four Pillars of Destiny. It combines a free chart calculator with plain-language guides, and it is run from South Korea by the creator behind the Korean YouTube channel Geobukwang (거북왕, "Turtle King"), whose videos are collected on the videos page.

What we publish

  • A free calculator. The Saju calculator builds the four pillars, Five Element balance, Ten Gods, luck cycles and symbolic stars. It runs in your browser. The interface is currently in Korean.
  • English guides. Our blog explains the system step by step, compares it with Western astrology and MBTI, and covers its place in Korean culture.
  • A lottery number generator. A separate, entertainment-only tool for Korean and world lotteries, which also has English pages such as the world lottery generator. It does not sell tickets and does not predict results.

Why we built it

Saju is a rich tradition, but most English material on it is either thin or copied from other sites. We wanted a resource that shows how a chart is really computed, explains where schools of thought disagree, and is honest about what the system can and cannot do.

Editorial standards

  • Original writing. Every article on this site is written for this site. We do not copy text from other websites or books.
  • Checked calculations. Sample charts in our articles are computed with the same engine as our calculator, and spot-checked against solar-term dates and the 60-cycle.
  • Honest framing. Saju is presented as a cultural and interpretive tradition. We state plainly that we are not aware of controlled evidence that it predicts personality or events.
  • No professional advice. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial, or relationship advice.
  • Disclosure. When we review our own tools we say so in the article.
  • Corrections. If you spot an error, please tell us through the contact page. We correct mistakes promptly and note significant changes in the article.

How the site is funded

The site is supported by advertising served by Google AdSense, which helps cover hosting and the domain. Advertising does not influence what we write, and we do not accept payment for reviews. See our privacy policy for details about cookies and ads.
