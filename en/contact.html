Contact Us

We read every message. You can reach us for:

  • Corrections to anything in our guides or calculator results
  • Questions about how a chart is calculated
  • Bug reports for the calculator or lottery tools
  • Partnership, collaboration and advertising inquiries

Please use the inquiry form on our contact and partnership page. It lets you send your name, email address and message directly to us. The page is in Korean, but you are welcome to write in English; the fields are Name, Company or brand, Email and Message.

Response time: we usually reply within a few business days. We cannot provide personal fortune readings or interpret your individual chart by email, but our step-by-step guide shows how to read it yourself.

Before you write

  • If you are reporting a chart error, include the birth date, time, calendar type (solar or lunar), the settings you used, and what result you expected. Birth details you send are used only to look into your report.
  • We never ask for passwords, payment details or government ID numbers.

Read how we handle information in our privacy policy, and learn more about the site on our About page.
